Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with account activity such as sign-in timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service: authenticating your account, saving and displaying your expenses, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and improving the reliability and features of Spendly. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us, after which your data will be permanently removed within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and database services, to operate the application. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not share your personal expense data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
